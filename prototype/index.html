What this prototype is testing

Four ideas, stripped of art and story so the loop can be judged on its own. Everything else — roster depth, characterisation, the run structure — is deliberately absent.

  • The hoard is one number doing three jobs. It is your currency, your income rate, and your lose condition at the same time. Every goblin you place is taken off the pile you are defending, and a smaller pile earns slower. Does spending feel tense or just punishing?
  • Heroes rob you, they don't kill you. Anything that reaches the pile loads up its pack and turns for the exit. Kill it on the way out and the coins fly home; let it reach the edge and they are gone for good. Rogues are the sharp version — they ignore your line entirely and walk straight through it, so walls do nothing and only damage stops them. Each lane has one bear trap as a last resort — it snaps once per run and then it is gone. Does the chase create good panic or bad frustration?
  • Junk fuses onto goblins. Bolt a torch onto a Chucker and you get a Firechucker. Add bees and you get something else again, with a new name and a new silhouette. Two items per goblin. Does finding a combination feel like discovery?
  • Curses stack with depth. Every third wave the cave itself gets worse, permanently, for the rest of the run. Does escalating the environment beat just adding more enemies?
