How it plays

Drag the chute while pouring. The stream keeps falling wherever you last pointed it, so a good pour is a steady hand across a whole run, not one lucky drop.

A standalone toy for the between-chamber idea: whatever gold you didn't spend gets poured down to the next chamber, through whatever the cave puts in the way. It is aimed, not spun — you steer the chute the whole way down, which is what stops it being a slot machine. The count is exact: while there is room on screen a multiplier makes real new coins, and once the shaft is full it raises the value of each coin instead, so the number stays honest when the picture cannot.
