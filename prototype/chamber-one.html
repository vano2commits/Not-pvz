Click a goblin, then another of the same kind, to merge.

One level, built for feel rather than scope. Every action answers back: placement thumps and kicks up dirt, throws wind up and recoil, and a merge is the loudest thing in the game. There is no passive income — every coin was carried in by somebody who died for it, so it spills out, bounces, and lies there until you pick it up. Screen shake is reserved for rare events; a rock landing moves the target, not the camera. All sound is synthesised live — no audio files — so every impact lands at a slightly different pitch.
