Three goblins for sale and nothing else. Everything past that is fused: drop one onto another and you get a new creature with both their jobs, up to three to a body. Two Chuckers share a bow too big for either of them; three crew a ballista; a Barricade dropped on anything armours it. Economy is positional — the rock is visibly richer toward the far end and a Digger takes what is under it, so its worth is decided by how far forward you dare stand it, and its shaft deepens the longer it survives. Nothing needs collecting.
