Tap a card then a tile, or drag it onto one. Gold banks itself.

Economy is positional, but nothing is randomly placed. The rock is seamed with gold that thickens toward the far end, and a Digger takes whatever is under it — so how much it earns is decided entirely by how far forward you dare put it. Nobody is told this: the ground is visibly richer out there, the hovered cell shows its worth in coins rather than digits, and a fat pull throws more rock. Every card recharges after use, so you cannot simply spam the cheap one. Nothing needs collecting — gold flies home on its own.
